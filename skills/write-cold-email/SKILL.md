# Write the first cold email

You are writing the first email of a three or four email sequence, for the
list built by `build-test-list` and the offer checked by `reframe-offer`.

## Step 1. Read

1. `./gtm-context/icp.md`: who is on the list.
2. `./gtm-context/offer.md`: the offer and why a stranger says yes.
3. `./gtm-context/proof.md`: one customer or number that matches this list.
4. `./gtm-context/voice.md`: how we sound and what we never say.
5. Call notes or transcripts in `./sources/` if they exist.

If `offer.md` does not pass the `reframe-offer` test, stop and say so.

## Step 2. Find the two asks

The close is a small yes, not a meeting. Find the two things buyers ask us to
send most often during the sales cycle (a model, a study, a sample, a price
sheet). Read them from the call notes. If there are no call notes, ask me:
"What do prospects ask you to send after a first call?"

Those two become ask A and ask B.

## Step 3. Write

Four parts, in this order:

1. **Who they are.** One line that shows the email is for accounts like
   theirs. Use the list's defining trait from `icp.md`.
2. **The problem, with a number.** What it costs them today.
3. **The offer and one proof.** What changes, and one customer like them or
   one number from `proof.md`.
4. **The ask.** One question. Write two versions, A and B.

Limits:

- Under 100 words, counting the body only.
- No request for a meeting or a call.
- No feature list. No adjectives about us.
- Words from `voice.md` that we never say do not appear.
- Subject line: under five words, lower case, about their outcome.

## Step 4. Hand back

- The subject line.
- The email, with ask A and ask B.
- The word count.
- Under the email, one line per sentence naming the file and line it came
  from.
- The follow-ups: one line each on what emails 2, 3 and 4 will add (a new
  proof, a new angle, a short last note). Do not write them yet.

## Rules

- Only the ask changes between A and B. Everything else stays identical, so
  the reply rate tells us which ask works.
- Do not invent a customer, a number or a study.
- If `proof.md` has nothing that matches this list, say so and ask me.
- Next: send, then `read-the-round`.
